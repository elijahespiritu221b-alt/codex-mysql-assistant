Who I am and what I do

An introduction to my interests, experience, and approach to web development.

I'm Elijah Espiritu, a student developing my skills in web development and information technology. I enjoy creating websites that combine a clear structure, thoughtful design, and useful functionality.

I have worked on a variety of personal coding and website projects. These projects have allowed me to experiment with layouts, animations, interactive elements, and different approaches to presenting information online.

I am open to learning from other developers, collaborating on suitable projects, and taking on challenges that help me grow.
